# Information on dataset `brain_size.csv`

**File name:** `brain_size.csv`

**Title:** Data on brain size (logged) for 1473 bird species

**Authors:** Rebecca Hooper, Becky Brett, and Alex Thornton

**Reference:** Hooper, R., Brett, B., & Thornton, A. (2022). Problems with using comparative analyses of avian brain size to test hypotheses of cognitive evolution. *PLoS One*, *17*(7), e0270771. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0270771>

**Source data DOI:** <https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.17081297>

**Date accessed:** 19th June 2026

**Dimensions:** 1473 rows x 9 columns

**Data dictionary:**

- `Species` - The scientific name (genus and species names) for the bird species.
- `GarciaPenaMLog` - Log brain volume (ml) for males of the bird species from García-Peña et al. (2013) <https://doi.org/10.1111/jeb.12104>
- `GarciaPenaFLog` - Log brain volume (ml) for females of the bird species from García-Peña et al. (2013) <https://doi.org/10.1111/jeb.12104>
- `GarciaPenaAveLog` - Log brain volume (ml) for the bird species, averaged across males and females, from García-Peña et al. (2013) <https://doi.org/10.1111/jeb.12104>
- `IwaniukArnoldLog` - Log brain volume (ml) for the bird species from Iwaniuk and Arnold (2004) <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1439-0310.2003.00957.x>
- `IwaniukNelsonLog` - Log brain volume (ml) for the bird species from Iwaniuk and Nelson (2003) <https://doi.org/10.1139/z03-190>
- `IwaniukDeanNelsonLog` - Log brain volume (ml) for the bird species from Iwaniuk, Dean, and Nelson (2005) <https://doi.org/10.1159/000081110>
- `SayolLog` - Log brain volume (ml) for the bird speies from Sayol et al. (2016) <https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms13971>
- `CorfieldLog` - Log brain volume (ml) for the bird species from Corfield et al. (2013) <https://doi.org/10.1159/000348834>
