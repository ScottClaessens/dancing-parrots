# Information on dataset `Tsuboi_etal_NEE_bird.xlsx`

**File name:** `Tsuboi_etal_NEE_bird.xlsx`

**Title:** Data on brain size, body size, and other variables for 10299 specimens of X bird species

**Authors:** Masahito Tsuboi et al.

**Reference:** Tsuboi, M., van der Bijl, W., Kopperud, B. T., Erritzøe, J., Voje, K. L., Kotrschal, A., ... & Kolm, N. (2018). Breakdown of brain–body allometry and the encephalization of birds and mammals. *Nature Ecology & Evolution*, *2*(9), 1492-1500. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-018-0632-1>

**Source data DOI:** <https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.6803276>

**Date accessed:** 13th August 2026

**Dimensions:** 10176 rows x 20 columns

**Data dictionary:**

- `Order` - Taxonomic order name.
- `Family` - Taxonomic family name.
- `Genus` - Taxonomic genus name.
- `Species` - Taxonomic species name.
- `Subspecies` - Taxonomic subspecies name.
- `Genus_Species` - Combined genus and species names.
- `Sex` - Sex of specimen, either male (m), female (f), or unknown.
- `Sample size` - Sample size of observation.
- `Brain Volume (ml)` - Brain volume of specimen in ml.
- `Method` - Method of brain size measurement, either volume (Vol) or mass (Mass).
- `Brain mass (g)` - Brain mass of specimen in grams.
- `Body mass (g)` - Body mass of specimen in grams.
- `Specimen ID` - Specimen ID code.
- `Museum` - Museum where the specimen was observed.
- `Date (mm-dd-yyyy)` - Date of observation.
- `Locality` - Locality of the observation.
- `Coordinate` - Longitude-latitude coordinate of the observation.
- `Age Class` - Age class of the specimen, either adult, subadult, or unknown.
- `Reference` - Reference for observation.
- `Remark` - Optional text remarks about the observation.
