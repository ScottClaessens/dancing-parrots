# Information on dataset `Data Sheet 3.xlsx`

**File name:** `Data Sheet 3.xlsx`

**Title:** Data on territoriality, social bonds, and communal signalling for 10329 bird species from Tobias et al. (2016)

**Authors:** Tobias et al.

**Reference:** Tobias, J. A., Sheard, C., Seddon, N., Meade, A., Cotton, A. J., & Nakagawa, S. (2016). Territoriality, social bonds, and the evolution of communal signaling in birds. *Frontiers in Ecology and Evolution*, *4*, 74. <https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2016.00074>

**Source data DOI:** <https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2016.00074>

**Date accessed:** 25th June 2026

**Dimensions:** 10329 rows x 13 columns

**Notes:** The dataset can be downloaded from the above link under "Supplementary materials". The data dictionary below was not supplied by the original authors.

**Data dictionary:**

- `GBD_SpeciesID` - Unique IDs for bird species
- `Order` - Taxonomic order for bird species
- `Family` - Taxonomic family for bird species
- `Species` - Species name
- `Communal` - Binary 0/1, presence of communal signalling in bird species
- `Uncertainy 1` - Ordinal measure of data quality for communal signalling (A = high quality data based on published sources or strongly supported evidence from direct observations; B = medium quality data, including cases where the classification is very likely correct but largely based on field observations and reports; C = low quality data based on few observations, or unsubstantiated literature reports; D = absence of direct evidence)
- `Duet` - Binary 0/1, presence of duets (communal signals involving two individuals)
- `Chorus` - Binary 0/1, presence of choruses (communal signals involving three or more individuals)
- `Social bond` - Ordinal measure of social bond stability (1 = solitary, 2 = short-term pair/group bond, 3 = long-term pair/group bond)
- `Territory` - Ordinal measure of degree of territoriality (1 = non-territorial, 2 = weakly territorial, 3 = year-round territorial)
- `Uncertainty 2` - Ordinal measure of data quality for ecological variables (A = high quality data based on published sources or strongly supported evidence from direct observations; B = medium quality data, including cases where the classification is very likely correct but largely based on field observations and reports; C = low quality data based on few observations, or unsubstantiated literature reports; D = absence of direct evidence)
- `Molecular` - Binary 0/1, presence of molecular data for species
- `Sources` - Sources used for data coding
